Day 17 — LAG and LEAD

The focus of Day 17 was learning how to use LAG() and LEAD() window functions to compare values across different rows in a dataset. These functions are commonly used for time-based analysis such as month-over-month comparisons and trend detection.

Tasks

Compare current month sales to the previous month
Aggregate monthly revenue and use the LAG() function to retrieve the previous month’s sales in order to compare performance across months.

Calculate month-over-month growth percentage
Using the previous month’s revenue, compute the percentage growth or decline in revenue between consecutive months.

Identify months with sales decline
Detect months where revenue decreased compared to the previous month by comparing current revenue with the value returned by LAG().
