Problem Statement

This task simulates real-world business decision-making using transactional sales data.

The objective was to move beyond descriptive reporting and answer three strategic business questions:

Which products are declining in sales?

Which customers are becoming inactive?

Which regions generate high revenue but low order volume?

These questions focus on performance risk detection across:

Products (portfolio performance)

Customers (retention risk)

Geography (market dynamics)

The goal was to apply time intelligence, window functions, benchmarking logic, and behavioral segmentation to generate actionable insights.
