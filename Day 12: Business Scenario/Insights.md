 Key Business Insights
1. Declining Products

Several products show negative Year-over-Year growth.

Declining trends suggest:

Reduced demand

Increased competition

Possible product lifecycle maturity

These products require:

Pricing review

Marketing support

Discontinuation evaluation (if persistent decline)

YoY analysis provides early warning signals before revenue impact becomes severe.

2. Inactive Customers

A portion of customers have not made purchases within the defined inactivity threshold.

These customers represent:

Churn risk

Lost revenue potential

Segmentation into lifecycle categories enables:

Targeted re-engagement campaigns

Loyalty incentives

Retention strategies

Customer inactivity analysis shifts focus from acquisition to retention.

3. High Revenue, Low Order Volume Regions

Certain regions generate above-average revenue but below-average order counts.

This suggests:

Higher average order values

Bulk purchasing behavior

Strong pricing power

These markets may be:

B2B-heavy

Premium-focused

High-value but sensitive to churn

Losing a small number of customers in these regions could significantly impact revenue.

 Strategic Takeaways

Revenue growth must be monitored at product level.

Customer recency is critical for churn prevention.

Regional performance should be evaluated beyond total revenue.

Benchmark-based filtering enables smarter decision-making than raw ranking.
