Day 16 — Running Totals

The focus of Day 16 was learning how to compute running totals (cumulative values) using SQL window functions. Running totals are useful for tracking how a metric grows over time or within a specific group.

Tasks

Calculate cumulative sales by month
Aggregate monthly sales and compute a running total ordered by year and month to observe how revenue accumulates over time.

Running total of sales per customer
Compute cumulative revenue for each customer by ordering their purchases chronologically. The running total resets for each customer.

Identify when cumulative sales crossed a threshold
Determine the point where cumulative sales within a product category exceed a defined benchmark (50% of the category’s total revenue). Return the earliest date when this threshold is reached.
