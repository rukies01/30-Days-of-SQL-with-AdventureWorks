Goal

The goal of this challenge was to analyze customer purchasing behavior from multiple analytical perspectives.

This included:

identifying repeat customers,
measuring the time gap between purchases,
and estimating customer lifetime value (CLV).

These are common business analytics problems used in:

retention analysis,
churn detection,
customer segmentation,
and revenue analysis.
