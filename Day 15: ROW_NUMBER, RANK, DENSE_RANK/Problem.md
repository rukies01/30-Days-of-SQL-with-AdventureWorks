Problem Statement

In real-world analytics, ranking is essential for identifying top performers, competitive positioning, and performance gaps.

The objective of this task was to apply SQL window functions to answer three key business questions:

Rank customers by total sales.

Rank products within each category.

Identify the top 3 products per category.

This exercise focuses on understanding the practical differences between:

ROW_NUMBER()

RANK()

DENSE_RANK()

and applying them in performance analysis scenarios.
