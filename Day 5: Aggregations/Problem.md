Day 5: Aggregations
Problems
Total sales per year.
Average order value per customer.
Count of orders per region.


The goal of this challenge was to practice using aggregate functions to summarize business data.

This included:

calculating yearly sales performance,
analyzing customer order behavior,
and measuring regional order activity.

These types of aggregations are commonly used in:

business reporting,
dashboard metrics,
sales analysis,
and operational summaries.
