1. Not All Growth Is Equal

A year with the most customers isn’t necessarily the most profitable year. Customer quality matters as much as quantity.

 2. Premium Product Strategy

By identifying products priced above their category average, we can:

Detect premium positioning

Analyze margin strategy

Evaluate whether high-priced products are driving revenue

 3. Customer Value Distribution

Finding customers above average spend highlights:

Potential VIP customers

Retention targets

Segmentation opportunities

 4. Cohort Thinking Changes Perspective

Cohort analysis shifts focus from:

“How much did we make this year?”
to

“How valuable were the customers we acquired that year?”

This is the foundation of:

Customer Lifetime Value (CLV)

Retention strategy

Marketing ROI analysis
