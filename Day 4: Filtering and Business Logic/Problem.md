Problem

The goal of Day 4 was to deepen my understanding of filtering logic, aggregation, and business-driven analysis using SQL.

The business questions explored were:

Find orders placed in the last 12 months (relative to the dataset).

Identify customers who placed more than 5 orders.

Show products with price above the average product price.

Show products whose price is above their category’s average price

The focus was on moving beyond basic filtering and applying SQL in ways that reflect real analytical decision-making.
