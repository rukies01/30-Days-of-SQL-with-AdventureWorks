Day 18 — Partitioning

The focus of Day 18 was understanding how to use PARTITION BY in window functions to perform calculations within specific groups without collapsing the dataset.

Tasks
1.Rank customers within each region
Calculate total revenue per customer and rank them within their respective regions based on revenue. This helps identify top-performing customers in each region.
2.Calculate average order value per customer compared to regional average
Compute the average order value (AOV) for each customer and compare it with the average AOV of their region. Classify customers as above, below, or equal to the regional benchmark.
3.Compare product performance within categories
Aggregate revenue at the product level and rank products within each category to evaluate their relative performance.
